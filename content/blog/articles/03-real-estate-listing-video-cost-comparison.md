The right choice depends on the listing, your timeline and how much the video needs to sell the feeling of the home.

## When is a videographer the right choice?

A videographer is the right choice when you need real footage that photos cannot capture, such as drone flights over a large estate, a walk-and-talk with the agent or interviews with the sellers. It is the most hands-on option.

The trade-offs are scheduling, time and add-ons. A shoot needs the home staged and the sellers out, weather can delay it and editing takes days. Scripts, voiceover and extra revisions often cost more.

## When is a slideshow app enough?

A slideshow app is enough for entry-level listings where speed and cost matter more than polish. It turns photos into a moving sequence with stock music in minutes.

For luxury homes, slideshows tend to undersell the property. High-end buyers have seen the same pan-and-zoom effect for years, and it signals an average marketing budget for a home that is anything but average.

## Which listing video option gives the best return?

The option with the best return is the one that brings qualified inquiries fastest for the price. For luxury listings, that usually means a video that tells a story and works in paid ads, because ads reach buyers outside your local market.

In our case studies, realtors who ran cinematic videos as Facebook ad campaigns closed their listings about twice as fast. Results vary by market, so track inquiries, showing requests and days on market for each listing.

## What should a listing video include, whatever the price?

Whatever you pay, make sure the video includes:

- A strong opening shot in the first two seconds.
- A vertical version for Reels and TikTok.
- Captions for silent viewing.
- Text on screen with the address, price and your brand.
- Music you are licensed to use in ads.
- At least one round of revisions.
