A winning luxury listing presentation shows sellers exactly how their home will be positioned, marketed and sold, with proof. The agents who win high-end listings arrive with a clear pricing story, a launch plan, real examples of their marketing and a preview of how the seller's own home could look on screen.

Here is a structure you can adapt for your next appointment.

## What do luxury sellers want from a listing agent?

Luxury sellers want an agent who understands what makes their home rare, can reach the right buyers wherever they are and will present the property at the level it deserves. Price and commission matter, but confidence in the marketing often decides the listing.

Many high-end sellers have bought and sold before. They have seen average marketing and know the difference.

## How should you structure a luxury listing presentation?

Structure it as a conversation in seven parts: listen, position, price, market, launch, prove and agree. Keep slides minimal and let your examples do the work.

1. **Listen.** Ask what they love about the home and what they will miss. Their words become your campaign.
2. **Position.** Share the one-sentence story of who the home is for. Use the formula from our [luxury marketing playbook](post:luxury-real-estate-marketing-guide).
3. **Price.** Walk through comparable sales and your pricing logic. See [how to value a luxury home](post:how-to-value-a-luxury-home).
4. **Market.** Show the asset kit: photos, cinematic video, reels, property page, ads.
5. **Launch.** Present a day-by-day plan for the first 14 days.
6. **Prove.** Share past listing videos, reach and results from your own sales.
7. **Agree.** Confirm next steps: photos, video, launch date.

## How do you talk about price with luxury sellers?

Talk about price with data and a clear strategy, not a single number. Luxury comps are often thin, so explain the range, the reasoning and how you will read the market in the first two weeks.

Sellers sometimes want to start high "to leave room." Explain that the first weeks bring the most attention, and an overpriced launch can waste them. Our article on [why luxury homes don't sell](post:luxury-home-not-selling) covers what happens when pricing and marketing miss.

## What marketing proof should you bring?

Bring proof the seller can see and feel: listing videos you produced, reels with real view counts, examples of property pages and results such as days on market or showing activity from your own listings. Real examples beat promises every time.

If you are newer to luxury, use your best work at any price point and show the plan you would follow.

## How can a preview video help you win the listing?

A preview video helps because it turns your marketing plan into something the seller experiences. Instead of saying "we'll create a beautiful video," you show them a short cinematic reel of their own home, before they sign.

Agents who do this change the conversation from commission to presentation. Sellers can picture the launch, share the preview with their family and feel confident about the result.

Use existing photos from a previous listing, or ask the seller for a few recent photos before the appointment.

## What should the launch plan include?

The launch plan should show what happens on each day of the first two weeks, who sees the home and how you will measure interest. Keep it to one page.

| Phase | Days | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Teaser | -7 to -1 | Coming-soon reel to your sphere and brokers |
| Premiere | 0 | Listing, video, property page and email go live together |
| Reach | 1 to 7 | Paid video ads aimed at likely buyers |
| Depth | 4 to 10 | Lifestyle and neighborhood cutdowns |
| Proof | 8 to 14 | Showing recap, broker open, price feedback |

## How do you handle commission objections?

Handle commission objections by tying your fee to the marketing and the outcome. Show what is included, from cinematic video to paid ads, and how that plan helps sell the home faster or for more.

When sellers see the exact value behind your fee, the conversation moves from "why so much?" to "when can we start?"

## What should you leave behind after the presentation?

Leave behind a printed or digital summary with your positioning statement, pricing range, launch plan and links to your example videos. If you made a preview video, send it the same day with a short personal note.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should a luxury listing presentation be?

Most work well in 45 to 60 minutes, with at least half of that spent listening and answering questions. Keep the formal part short and visual.

### Should I bring a slide deck?

A short deck helps structure the meeting, but keep it to a few slides. Video examples and the launch plan matter more than slides full of text.

### What if I don't have luxury sales yet?

Show your best marketing at any price, a strong plan and examples of the quality you will deliver. A preview video of their home is especially useful when you are building a track record.

### How do I follow up after a listing appointment?

Follow up within 24 hours with a thank-you note, the summary and any preview video. Answer every open question in writing so the seller can share it with family or advisors.
