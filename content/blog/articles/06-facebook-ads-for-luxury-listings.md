Facebook ads for luxury listings work best with a short cinematic video, a clear landing page and a campaign set up in Meta's housing Special Ad Category. Run the campaign during the first two weeks on market, retarget people who watched the video and measure inquiries, not clicks.

This guide walks through each step for Facebook and Instagram, which run on the same Meta ads platform.

## Can you run Facebook ads for real estate listings?

Yes, but housing ads must run under Meta's Special Ad Category for housing. This exists to support fair housing, so it restricts targeting options such as age, gender and detailed location targeting. Always follow fair housing laws and Meta's current advertising policies.

The practical effect is that you cannot narrowly target "wealthy buyers." Instead, the ad itself must attract them, which is why the creative matters so much for luxury.

## What kind of ad creative works for luxury listings?

Short, cinematic vertical video works best. It stops the scroll, shows how the home feels and naturally attracts people who are drawn to that lifestyle. A good luxury ad has a striking first shot, captions, a short line of copy and one clear action.

| Element | What works |
|---|---|
| Format | Vertical 9:16 video, 15 to 30 seconds |
| Opening | The best view or most unusual feature in the first two seconds |
| Sound | Original music and voiceover, with captions |
| Copy | One or two lines about the lifestyle, then price or address |
| Call to action | "Learn more" or "Book a private showing" |

## How do you set up a luxury listing campaign step by step?

1. **Choose the objective.** For new listings, choose a traffic or leads objective. For awareness of a teaser, video views can work.
2. **Select the housing Special Ad Category.** This is required for real estate.
3. **Set your location.** Target the metro area and, if your buyers often relocate, the cities they come from.
4. **Add your creative.** Upload the vertical video plus a square or horizontal version for other placements.
5. **Write short copy.** Lead with the feeling, then the facts.
6. **Send traffic to one page.** A property page with the video, photos and a simple inquiry form.
7. **Set a launch budget.** Spend more in the first 14 days, when interest is highest.
8. **Set up retargeting.** Build audiences of people who watched most of the video or visited the page.

## How much should you spend on Facebook ads for a luxury listing?

There is no single right number. Many agents set a fixed launch budget per listing and spend most of it in the first two weeks, then keep a smaller retargeting budget running until the home sells. Start with an amount you are comfortable testing and adjust based on the cost per qualified inquiry.

## How does retargeting help sell luxury homes?

Retargeting shows follow-up ads to people who already showed interest, such as watching most of your video or visiting the property page. These viewers are far more likely to inquire, and luxury buyers often need several touches before they reach out.

Good retargeting ads include a different scene from the home, a neighborhood reel or an invitation to a private showing.

## What should you measure in a luxury ad campaign?

Measure the actions that lead to a sale:

- Video completion rate (how much of the video people watch).
- Landing page visits and time on page.
- Inquiries and showing requests.
- Cost per qualified inquiry.
- Days on market compared with similar listings.

In our case studies, realtors who ran cinematic videos as Facebook ad campaigns closed listings about twice as fast. Your results will depend on your market and price point, so compare each campaign with your own past listings.

## What are common mistakes with luxury real estate ads?

- **Boosting a photo post.** A single photo rarely shows what makes a luxury home special.
- **Skipping the Special Ad Category.** Ads can be rejected or disapproved.
- **Sending traffic to a busy portal page.** Use a clean page focused on one home.
- **Stopping after a week.** Luxury buyers move slowly. Keep retargeting running.
- **Writing feature lists.** Lead with lifestyle, then facts.

## Can Facebook ads help you win more listings too?

Yes. Running visible, high-quality ads shows sellers in your market that you invest in marketing. Mention your ad strategy in your [listing presentation](post:luxury-listing-presentation) and show past campaign results.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Facebook ads effective for luxury real estate?

They can be, especially with strong video and retargeting. Meta's reach makes it possible to show a home to buyers in other cities who are planning a move.

### What is the housing Special Ad Category?

It is a Meta setting required for housing ads. It limits certain targeting options to help prevent discrimination, in line with fair housing laws.

### Should I run ads on Instagram too?

Yes. Facebook and Instagram ads run from the same Meta ads platform, and vertical video performs well in Instagram Reels and Stories placements.

### How long should a luxury listing ad run?

Run the main campaign through the first two weeks, then keep retargeting active until the home is under contract. Refresh the creative every couple of weeks so it does not go stale.
