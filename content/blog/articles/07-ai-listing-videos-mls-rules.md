AI listing videos are safe to use when every scene stays true to the real home. That means no invented windows, finishes, views or rooms, clear disclosure where your MLS or brokerage requires it and a scene-by-scene check against the original photos before you publish.

Rules differ between MLSs and brokerages, so always check your own. This guide covers the principles that apply almost everywhere.

## Can you use AI-generated video for real estate listings?

Yes, many agents now use AI-assisted video in their marketing. The question is not whether AI was used, but whether the result accurately represents the property. Buyers rely on listing media to make decisions, so anything that changes what the home actually has is a problem.

Some MLSs restrict video on the listing itself or have specific rules for virtually staged or enhanced media. Check yours before uploading, and use social media and your property page where MLS rules are stricter.

## What can go wrong with AI listing videos?

AI video models are trained to make things look beautiful, and sometimes they fill gaps with details that are not there. Common errors include:

- Extra windows or doors.
- Different countertops, floors or fixtures.
- A view that does not match the real one.
- Rooms that are larger or shaped differently.
- Furniture appearing or disappearing.

On social media, that is a glitch. On a listing, it can be misrepresentation.

## How do you keep an AI listing video truthful?

Keep it truthful by starting from real photos, limiting how much the AI changes, reviewing every scene against the original and redoing anything that does not match. Here is the process we follow at Wonder Ads.

1. **Start from the real MLS photos.** Every scene begins with a photo of the actual room.
2. **Use gentle motion in detail-heavy rooms.** Kitchens, bathrooms and built-ins get subtle camera movement so the AI has less room to invent.
3. **Check side by side.** Compare layout, fixtures, counters, windows and views with the source photo.
4. **Redo what doesn't match.** Any scene with an invented detail is regenerated or removed.
5. **Keep disclosures.** If a photo was virtually staged and labeled, the label stays in.
6. **Share the proof.** Provide a scene-by-scene comparison the agent can show to their broker or seller.

## Do you have to disclose AI-enhanced listing media?

Disclosure rules vary. Many MLSs and brokerages already require labels on virtually staged photos, and some are adding guidance for AI-enhanced media. When in doubt, disclose. A short note such as "Video includes AI-enhanced motion created from listing photos" is easy and builds trust.

This is not legal advice. Your MLS rules, brokerage policy and local regulations decide what is required.

## What about people in AI listing videos?

Adding people, such as a couple enjoying the terrace, can help buyers picture the lifestyle. It does not misrepresent the home as long as the property itself stays accurate. Keep casting and scenes consistent with fair housing guidelines, and avoid anything that suggests a preference for certain buyers.

## Which rooms need the most care?

| Room or feature | Why it needs care | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchens | Many small details: hardware, appliances, counters | Subtle motion, close check of fixtures |
| Bathrooms | Tiles, fixtures, mirrors | Minimal movement |
| Windows and views | Easy for AI to add or change | Compare window count and view |
| Exteriors | Rooflines, landscaping, neighbors | Check structure and surroundings |
| Floor plans and room size | Wide-angle motion can distort space | Avoid exaggerated movement |

## How do you explain AI video to sellers and brokers?

Explain it simply: the video is built from the real listing photos, adds camera movement and atmosphere and is checked scene by scene so nothing is invented. Then show the comparison sheet. Most sellers are excited, and brokers appreciate the documentation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AI real estate video legal?

Using AI tools is generally fine. What matters is accuracy and following your MLS, brokerage and local advertising rules. Misrepresenting a property is the risk to avoid.

### Can I upload an AI listing video to the MLS?

It depends on your MLS. Some allow branded or unbranded video links, some restrict enhanced media. Check the current rules or ask your MLS directly.

### Does AI video replace professional photography?

No. Great AI video depends on great photos. See our [luxury real estate photography checklist](post:luxury-real-estate-photography-checklist).

### How do I know an AI video is accurate?

Ask for a scene-by-scene comparison with the original photos. If the provider cannot show one, review the video yourself before publishing.
