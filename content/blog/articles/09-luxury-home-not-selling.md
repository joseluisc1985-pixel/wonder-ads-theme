A luxury home usually stops selling for one of three reasons: the price is out of line with the market, the marketing doesn't show why the home is special or the home isn't reaching the right buyers. The fix is a relaunch: reset the price strategy, refresh the presentation with new photos and cinematic video and push it to buyers in and outside your market.

Here are nine fixes, in the order we would try them.

## Why do luxury homes sit on the market?

Luxury homes sit on the market because there are fewer buyers at higher price points, and those buyers are selective. When a home is overpriced, presented like an average listing or marketed only locally, it can quietly fall off buyers' radar.

Time on market also feeds on itself. The longer a listing sits, the more buyers wonder what is wrong with it.

## Fix 1: Is the price right for today's market?

Review the price against recent comparable sales, active competition and buyer feedback. If the home has had showings but no offers, price is often part of the story. Our guide to [how to value a luxury home](post:how-to-value-a-luxury-home) explains why comps get tricky at the top of the market.

A price change works best when paired with fresh marketing, so buyers see a new listing rather than a discount.

## Fix 2: Does the marketing show why the home is special?

If the photos and description could describe any home in the area, buyers have no reason to care. Rewrite the positioning around the one thing that makes the home rare, then rebuild the marketing around it.

## Fix 3: Refresh the photos

Reshoot in better light, restyle key rooms and add twilight exteriors. Fresh images also reset how the listing appears in portals and social feeds.

## Fix 4: Add a cinematic listing video

A cinematic video gives the listing a new first impression and a shareable asset for social media and ads. Show the lifestyle: mornings on the terrace, evenings by the pool. See our guide to [luxury real estate video marketing](post:luxury-real-estate-video-marketing).

## Fix 5: Run paid video ads

Paid ads put the home in front of buyers who are not actively searching portals, including people planning a move from another city. Use the vertical video as the main ad and retarget viewers. Our [Facebook ads guide for luxury listings](post:facebook-ads-for-luxury-listings) walks through setup.

## Fix 6: Reach relocating and second-home buyers

Many luxury buyers come from outside the area. Share the listing with agents in the cities your buyers usually come from, and target ads to those markets.

## Fix 7: Improve showings

Check the basics: lighting, temperature, scent, clutter and how easy it is to schedule. Provide a printed brochure and a QR code to the video so buyers can rewatch it later and share it with their partner.

## Fix 8: Collect and act on feedback

Ask every showing agent for honest feedback, then group it into price, condition and presentation. If the same comment keeps coming up, address it directly.

## Fix 9: Relaunch like it's new

Bundle the changes into a clear relaunch: new photos, new video, any price update and a fresh campaign, all going live on the same day. A coordinated relaunch gets more attention than small changes made one at a time.

| Symptom | Likely cause | First fix |
|---|---|---|
| Few showings | Marketing or reach | New video and paid ads |
| Showings, no offers | Price or condition | Price review and feedback |
| Lots of views, few inquiries | Unclear story or weak call to action | Repositioning and clearer next step |
| Interest from far away, none locally | Local market is quiet | Target relocating buyers |

## How do you talk to sellers about a stale listing?

Be direct and bring data. Show views, showings and feedback, then present the relaunch plan. Sellers usually respond well to a clear plan that combines a fresh presentation with a realistic price.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is too long for a luxury home to be on the market?

It depends on the price point and local market. If showings and inquiries have slowed well below similar homes, it is time to review price and marketing rather than wait.

### Should I take the listing off the market and relist it?

Some agents do this to reset days on market. Rules vary by MLS, so check yours. A real relaunch with new marketing matters more than the relisting itself.

### Does a price reduction hurt a luxury listing?

A well-timed price change paired with fresh marketing can bring new buyers. Small, repeated reductions without new marketing tend to work less well.

### Can a new video really help a stale listing?

A new video gives buyers a new reason to look and gives you fresh content for ads and social media. It works best as part of a full relaunch.
