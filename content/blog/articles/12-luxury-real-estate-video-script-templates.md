A great real estate video script for a luxury listing is short, specific and emotional. For a 30-second video, aim for roughly 60 to 75 words: an opening line that sets a feeling, two or three lines about life in the home and a closing line with the address or a call to action.

Below are five templates you can copy and adapt.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Keep 30-second scripts to about 60 to 75 words, read slowly.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, natural pauses.
- Describe moments and feelings, then add facts on screen.
- Use one consistent, warm tone from start to finish.
- End with a clear next step.
</div>

## How long should a real estate video script be?

A 30-second video needs about 60 to 75 words if the voiceover leaves room for pauses and music. A 60-second video can carry roughly 120 to 150 words. Read it aloud with a timer, and cut until it breathes.

## What makes a luxury listing script work?

It works when it sounds like a person talking, not a brochure. Lead with a feeling, name specific moments and let the visuals and on-screen text carry the specs.

Rules we follow:

- One idea per sentence.
- Specific images ("coffee on the terrace") over vague claims ("luxurious living").
- A steady, warm tone. Avoid swinging from soft to hype.
- Pauses between lines so the music can breathe.

## Template 1: Waterfront home

> Some mornings start with an alarm. Here, they start with the water.
> Coffee on the terrace. The boat waiting at the dock.
> Long lunches in the shade, and evenings that end with the city lights across the bay.
> [Home name], [neighborhood]. Life, closer to the water.

*On screen: address, price, beds/baths, dock length.*

## Template 2: Penthouse

> Above the city, everything slows down.
> Morning light fills every room. Dinner comes with a skyline view.
> And at night, the whole city sparkles just for you.
> [Building], residence [number]. The view is only the beginning.

*On screen: floor, square footage, terrace size, price.*

## Template 3: Family estate

> Every family has a place where the memories happen.
> Saturday breakfasts around the island. Cannonballs into the pool.
> Friends who stay a little longer, because there's room for everyone.
> [Address]. A home made for the moments that matter.

*On screen: bedrooms, lot size, schools nearby, price.*

## Template 4: New construction

> Designed for the way we live now.
> Clean lines. Natural light. Every detail chosen with care.
> Room to work, room to gather and room to simply breathe.
> [Project name], [neighborhood]. Brand new, and ready for your story.

*On screen: builder, completion date, specs, price.*

## Template 5: Coming-soon teaser (15 seconds)

> Something special is coming to [neighborhood].
> A home with a view you'll want to wake up to.
> Coming soon. Follow for the premiere.

*On screen: "Coming soon" and your name.*

## How do you choose the right voiceover tone?

Match the tone to the home and buyer. Waterfront and family homes suit a warm tone. New construction and penthouses can take a cleaner, more focused delivery. Energetic delivery works for younger buyers and investment properties. Whatever you choose, keep it consistent throughout.

| Tone | Best for |
|---|---|
| Warm | Family estates, waterfront, legacy homes |
| Focused | Penthouses, new construction, design-led homes |
| Energetic | Investment properties, younger buyers, social teasers |

## Should every listing video have a voiceover?

A voiceover makes the video feel like a story, and captions let silent viewers follow along. Some agents prefer music only, which can work for short teasers. For a hero video, a short voiceover usually connects more strongly with buyers.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many words is a 30-second script?

About 60 to 75 words for a relaxed pace with pauses. Faster delivery can fit more, but luxury videos usually benefit from a slower rhythm.

### Should I mention the price in the voiceover?

Usually put the price on screen instead. It keeps the voiceover focused on the feeling while still giving buyers the facts.

### Can I use AI voices for listing videos?

Yes, many agents do. Choose a natural, warm voice and keep the pacing slow so it sounds human.

### Who should read the voiceover?

A professional voice, an AI voice or you. If you voice it yourself, record in a quiet room and read slowly.
