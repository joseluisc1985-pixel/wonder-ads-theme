The U.S. luxury real estate market is still rising in 2026, but unevenly. According to Redfin, the median luxury home sold for about $1.39 million in the three months ending April 2026, up 3.6% from a year earlier, with pending luxury sales up 4.3%. Gains are strongest in Sun Belt metros like Tampa and Las Vegas, while New York and Denver saw small declines.

Here is what the latest data shows and what it means if you list or sell high-end homes. *Figures reflect the latest reports available at the time of writing. We update this page as new data comes out.*

- The U.S. median luxury sale price was about $1.39 million, up 3.6% year over year (Redfin, three months ending April 2026).
- Luxury pending sales rose 4.3%, new listings rose 2.0% and active listings rose 1.4%.
- Tampa (+17.1%), Las Vegas (+16.1%) and Kansas City (+15.2%) led luxury price growth.
- South Florida's high-end sales jumped in Q2 2026, led by Miami-Dade single-family homes.

## How is the luxury real estate market doing in 2026?

The luxury market is growing steadily rather than booming. Redfin defines luxury homes as the top 5% of each metro by estimated value. In the three months ending April 2026, their median sale price rose 3.6% to about $1.39 million, and pending luxury sales rose 4.3% year over year.

Supply is loosening slowly. Luxury new listings rose 2.0% and active luxury listings rose 1.4%, a smaller increase than in the non-luxury market (2.3%).

|---|---|

| Pending sales | +4.3% |
| New listings | +2.0% |
| Active listings | +1.4% |

## Where are luxury home prices rising fastest?

Luxury prices rose most in Tampa (17.1%), Las Vegas (16.1%) and Kansas City (15.2%) over the same period, according to Redfin. Prices fell in only four major metros: Detroit (-2.4%), Cincinnati (-1.6%), New York (-0.6%) and Denver (-0.6%).

For agents, the takeaway is local. National averages hide big differences between markets, so your pricing and marketing should reflect what is happening in your metro.

## Is the gap between luxury and regular homes growing?

Yes. Redfin reported in July 2026 that the typical luxury home costs 3.6 times as much as a typical non-luxury home, up from 3.5 a year earlier. The widest gaps were in West Palm Beach (8.9 times) and Miami (8.8 times, up from 7.6), followed by New York, Los Angeles and Phoenix.

## What is happening in South Florida's luxury market?

South Florida is one of the strongest luxury regions in the country right now. The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties' Q2 2026 Luxury Market Report found that high-end single-family sales across its five-county region rose 20.1% to 8,013, with the average price up 7.2% to $2.63 million.

Miami-Dade stood out: luxury single-family transactions rose 32.5% to 1,000 and the average price jumped 21.9% to $3.61 million. ## How does the U.S. compare with global prime markets?

Globally, prime prices are rising modestly. Knight Frank's Prime Global Cities Index showed average annual growth of 2.6% in Q2 2026. Among U.S. cities in the index, San Francisco rose 5.0%, Miami 3.9% and Los Angeles 1.4%, while New York slipped 0.4%. Tokyo led the world with 50.7%.

## What do these trends mean for luxury agents?

They mean buyers are active, but they are selective and spread across markets. Three practical moves:

1. **Price from local data, not national headlines.** A 17% gain in Tampa says little about New York.
2. **Market to relocating buyers.** Strong demand in the Sun Belt and Florida often comes from out of state.
3. **Win attention fast.** With more listings coming to market, presentation and launch speed matter more.

Sellers should know that demand is healthy in many markets, but buyers have more choice than a year ago as listings slowly rise. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the average price of a luxury home in the U.S.?

Redfin put the median U.S. luxury home sale price at about $1.39 million in the three months ending April 2026. Luxury thresholds vary widely by metro. See [what is considered a luxury home](post:what-is-considered-a-luxury-home).

### Is the luxury housing market slowing down?

Not overall. Prices and pending sales were both up year over year in Redfin's latest data, though a few metros, including New York and Denver, saw small price declines.

### Which U.S. cities have the hottest luxury markets?

By luxury price growth, Tampa, Las Vegas and Kansas City led in Redfin's latest report. By sales activity, South Florida markets like Miami-Dade posted strong gains in Q2 2026.

### Will luxury home prices go up in 2027?

No one can say for certain. Prices depend on interest rates, the stock market, migration and local supply. Watch local data and quarterly reports, and plan for different scenarios.
