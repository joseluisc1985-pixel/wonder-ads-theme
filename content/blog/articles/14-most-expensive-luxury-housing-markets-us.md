Among the major metros in Redfin's latest luxury report, the most expensive luxury housing markets in the U.S. in 2026 are Miami, Los Angeles, West Palm Beach and New York, where the typical luxury home sells for roughly $4.4 million to $4.9 million. According to Redfin's data for the three months ending May 2026, Miami's median luxury home was about $4.86 million, the highest of the metros in its report.

Here is the full picture, including where the gap between luxury and regular homes is widest.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Miami had the highest median luxury price in Redfin's report at about $4.86 million.
- Los Angeles (about $4.51M), West Palm Beach (about $4.51M) and New York (about $4.38M) followed.
- West Palm Beach and Miami have the widest luxury premiums, close to 9 times a typical home.
- Miami's premium jumped the most in a year, from 7.6 to 8.8 times.
- Redfin defines luxury as the top 5% of each metro's homes by estimated value.
</div>

## Which U.S. metros have the most expensive luxury homes?

Based on Redfin's July 2026 report (three months ending May 2026), these metros had the highest median luxury prices among those it highlighted:

| Metro | Median luxury price | Median non-luxury price | Luxury premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Miami, FL | $4,855,331 | $554,441 | 8.8x |
| Los Angeles, CA | $4,512,482 | $916,294 | 4.9x |
| West Palm Beach, FL | $4,510,196 | $506,609 | 8.9x |
| New York, NY | $4,380,249 | $789,723 | 5.5x |
| Phoenix, AZ | $2,225,630 | $460,574 | 4.8x |

*Luxury = top 5% of homes in each metro by estimated value. Non-luxury = 35th to 65th percentile. Source: Redfin.*

## What is the "luxury premium," and why does it matter?

The luxury premium is how many times more a typical luxury home costs than a typical mid-market home in the same metro. Nationally, it was 3.6 times in Redfin's latest data, up from 3.5 a year earlier.

A high premium means the top of the market is pulling away from everyone else. In West Palm Beach and Miami, luxury homes cost almost nine times what a typical home does, which reflects both wealthy buyers moving in and the scarcity of prime waterfront property.

## Where is the luxury premium growing fastest?

Miami's premium grew the most, from 7.6 to 8.8 times in a year. Tampa (3.8 to 4.4), Phoenix (4.3 to 4.8) and Nashville and Philadelphia (each up 0.2 to 4.6) also saw notable increases, according to Redfin.

Florida appears twice in that list, which lines up with strong luxury sales activity in the state. See our [Miami luxury market report](post:miami-luxury-real-estate-market-2026) for more.

## Where is luxury real estate most affordable?

Among major metros, the smallest luxury premiums were in Portland, Oregon (2.6 times, with a median luxury price of about $1.46 million), Columbus, Ohio (2.9 times, about $1.02 million) and Sacramento (3.0 times, about $1.72 million). In these markets, the jump from a typical home to a luxury home is much smaller.

## What does this mean for luxury agents?

It means "luxury" looks very different from city to city. A $1.5 million home is solidly luxury in Columbus and mid-market in Los Angeles. Price thresholds, buyer expectations and marketing budgets should all reflect your local market.

Practical takeaways:

- **Know your metro's luxury threshold.** See [what is considered a luxury home](post:what-is-considered-a-luxury-home).
- **Match the marketing to the price point.** Buyers at $4 million expect cinematic presentation.
- **Reach relocating buyers.** High-premium markets like Miami draw many out-of-state buyers.

## Are luxury prices still rising in the most expensive markets?

Mostly, but not everywhere. Redfin's May 2026 report showed luxury prices falling slightly in New York (-0.6%), while Florida and Sun Belt markets kept climbing. Read our [2026 luxury market report](post:luxury-real-estate-market-2026) for the national view.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the most expensive luxury housing market in the U.S.?

In Redfin's July 2026 report, Miami had the highest median luxury price at about $4.86 million among the metros highlighted. Individual neighborhoods elsewhere can be far more expensive.

### How does Redfin define a luxury home?

Redfin defines luxury homes as those in the top 5% of their metro by estimated market value.

### Why is Miami's luxury premium so high?

A strong flow of wealthy buyers, limited waterfront land and a wide gap between prime and typical homes all contribute. Miami's premium rose from 7.6 to 8.8 times in a year.

### Is New York still a top luxury market?

Yes. New York's median luxury price was about $4.38 million, among the highest in the country, even though luxury prices dipped slightly year over year.
