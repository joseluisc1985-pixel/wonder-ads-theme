Miami's luxury real estate market is one of the strongest in the country in 2026. In Q2 2026, luxury single-family sales in Miami-Dade rose 32.5% year over year to 1,000 transactions, and the average price climbed 21.9% to $3.61 million, according to The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties. Redfin also ranks Miami's luxury premium among the widest in the U.S.

Here is what is driving the market and what it means for agents. *Figures reflect the latest reports available at the time of writing.*

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Miami-Dade luxury single-family sales rose 32.5% to 1,000 in Q2 2026, at an average price of $3.61 million (+21.9%).
- Luxury condo sales rose 11.2% to 607, at an average of $2.83 million (+4.9%).
- Miami's median luxury home was about $4.86 million in Redfin's data, the highest of the metros it highlighted.
- The typical Miami luxury home costs 8.8 times a typical home, up from 7.6 a year earlier.
- Knight Frank recorded 3.9% annual prime price growth for Miami in Q2 2026.
</div>

## How is the Miami luxury market performing in 2026?

Strongly, especially for single-family homes. The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties' Q2 2026 Luxury Market Report showed big gains in both transactions and prices in Miami-Dade, and the report noted that the surge did not depend on seller price reductions.

| Miami-Dade, Q2 2026 | Transactions | Change | Average price | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luxury single-family | 1,000 | +32.5% | $3.61M | +21.9% |
| Luxury condos | 607 | +11.2% | $2.83M | +4.9% |

*Source: The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties, Q2 2026 Luxury Market Report, via RISMedia.*

## How expensive are luxury homes in Miami?

Very. Redfin's July 2026 report put Miami's median luxury home (top 5% of the metro) at about $4.86 million for the three months ending May 2026, compared with about $554,000 for a typical mid-market home. That 8.8 times gap was up from 7.6 times a year earlier, the biggest increase among major metros.

## Are Miami luxury prices still going up?

Yes, by most measures. Average luxury single-family prices in Miami-Dade rose 21.9% year over year in Q2 2026, and Knight Frank's Prime Global Cities Index showed Miami prime prices up 3.9% over the year to Q2 2026. Different reports measure different slices of the market, so growth rates vary by source.

## Single-family homes or condos: where is the demand?

Single-family homes are leading. Luxury house sales grew almost three times faster than luxury condo sales in Miami-Dade in Q2 2026 (32.5% versus 11.2%), and average house prices rose much faster too. Waterfront single-family homes, in particular, are scarce, which supports prices.

Condos remain an important part of the market, especially for second-home and international buyers. Our article on [pre-construction luxury condos](post:pre-construction-luxury-condos) covers that side.

## Who is buying luxury homes in Miami?

Miami draws domestic buyers relocating from higher-tax states, second-home buyers and international buyers. Florida has no state personal income tax, which is one reason the state attracts high earners and retirees. Lifestyle, climate and a growing business scene play a big role too.

For agents, this means many buyers start their search from another city or country, often on their phones. Video and social media matter even more when buyers can't easily tour in person.

## Which Miami areas are most sought after for luxury?

Waterfront and island neighborhoods traditionally lead the luxury market, along with established areas known for large lots, top schools and privacy. Demand also follows new development and lifestyle hubs. Inventory and pricing differ sharply by neighborhood, so local comps matter more than metro averages.
