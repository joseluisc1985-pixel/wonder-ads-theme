Palm Beach and Miami are both top-tier luxury markets, but they suit different buyers. Miami offers a big-city lifestyle with fast-rising luxury prices, while Palm Beach County delivers a quieter, estate-focused market with higher luxury sales volume. In Q2 2026, Palm Beach County recorded 2,534 luxury single-family sales averaging $2.89 million, while Miami-Dade recorded 1,000 averaging $3.61 million.

Here is how they compare in detail. *Figures reflect the latest reports available at the time of writing.*

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Palm Beach County had more than twice as many luxury single-family sales as Miami-Dade in Q2 2026.
- Miami-Dade's average luxury house price rose much faster (+21.9% vs. +4.6%).
- Both metros have luxury premiums near 9 times a typical home, the widest in the U.S.
- Miami suits buyers who want city energy. Palm Beach suits buyers who want privacy and estate living.
- Palm Beach luxury condo prices dipped 7.1% while Miami's rose 4.9%.
</div>

## How do Palm Beach and Miami luxury prices compare?

Miami is more expensive at the top, and prices there are rising faster. Here is the side-by-side from The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties' Q2 2026 Luxury Market Report, and Redfin's metro-level data.

| Q2 2026 | Miami-Dade | Palm Beach County |
|---|---|---|
| Luxury single-family sales | 1,000 (+32.5%) | 2,534 (+24.2%) |
| Average luxury house price | $3.61M (+21.9%) | $2.89M (+4.6%) |
| Luxury condo sales | 607 (+11.2%) | 770 (+26.6%) |
| Average luxury condo price | $2.83M (+4.9%) | $2.39M (-7.1%) |
| Median luxury price, metro (Redfin) | About $4.86M (Miami) | About $4.51M (West Palm Beach) |
| Luxury premium vs. typical home (Redfin) | 8.8x | 8.9x |

*Sources: The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties via RISMedia; Redfin, three months ending May 2026.*

## Which market has more luxury sales?

Palm Beach County does. It recorded 2,534 luxury single-family sales in Q2 2026, compared with 1,000 in Miami-Dade, and more luxury condo sales as well. For agents, that means more transaction opportunities in Palm Beach, with Miami offering higher average prices.

## What is the lifestyle difference between Palm Beach and Miami?

Miami is a global city: dining, nightlife, art, business and a fast pace. Palm Beach is quieter and more residential, known for estates, golf, private clubs and a seasonal social calendar. Both offer waterfront living and Florida's lack of a state personal income tax.

| | Miami | Palm Beach |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | Fast, urban | Relaxed, residential |
| Typical luxury product | Waterfront homes, high-rise condos | Estates, waterfront homes, golf communities |
| Draw | Culture, business, nightlife | Privacy, clubs, golf, beaches |
| Buyer profile | Younger wealth, international, relocating professionals | Established families, retirees, seasonal residents |

## Which buyers are best suited to each market?

Miami suits buyers who want to live in a city and value walkability, culture and a global feel. Palm Beach suits buyers who want space, privacy and an established, slower lifestyle. Many luxury buyers look at both before deciding, which is why lifestyle marketing matters so much in South Florida.

It depends on your goals and risk tolerance. Miami has shown faster recent price growth in luxury single-family homes, while Palm Beach offers deeper sales volume. In Miami, lead with lifestyle and the city: views, dining, culture and the energy of the neighborhood. In Palm Beach, lead with privacy, space and the rhythm of daily life: golf mornings, long lunches, quiet evenings. In both, reach out-of-state buyers with video and paid ads.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Palm Beach more expensive than Miami?

At the metro level, Miami's median luxury price (about $4.86M) was slightly higher than West Palm Beach's (about $4.51M) in Redfin's data. Specific Palm Beach neighborhoods can be far more expensive.

### Which market is growing faster?

In Q2 2026, Miami-Dade's average luxury single-family price grew 21.9% versus 4.6% in Palm Beach County, per The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties.

### Do both markets have no state income tax?

Yes. Florida does not have a state personal income tax, which applies to both Miami and Palm Beach.

### Can one agent cover both markets?

Many South Florida agents work across counties, but local knowledge matters. Neighborhood-level pricing and buyer expectations differ a lot between the two.
