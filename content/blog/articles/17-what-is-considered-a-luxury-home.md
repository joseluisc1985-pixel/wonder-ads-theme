A luxury home is usually defined as a property in the top 5% to 10% of prices in its local market. There is no single national dollar threshold: Redfin, for example, defines luxury as the top 5% of each metro by estimated value, which works out to about $1 million in Columbus, Ohio, but more than $4.8 million in Miami.

Beyond price, luxury homes share a mix of location, quality, privacy and lifestyle features.

## How is a luxury home defined?

Most data providers define luxury by price rank within a local market. Redfin uses the top 5% of homes in each metro by estimated market value. Other analysts use the top 10% or a fixed local price floor. Because home values vary so much between cities, a percentile is more useful than a single national number.

## What price is considered luxury in the U.S.?

Nationally, Redfin put the median luxury home sale price at about $1.39 million for the three months ending April 2026. In individual metros, the typical luxury home ranged widely:

| Los Angeles, CA | About $4.51M |

*Three months ending May 2026. Source: Redfin.*

## What features make a home luxury?

Location comes first, then quality and experience. Common features of luxury homes include:

- **Prime location:** waterfront, views, top neighborhoods, privacy.
- **Architecture and design:** a recognized architect or builder, custom layouts.
- **Materials:** natural stone, custom millwork, high-end appliances.
- **Outdoor living:** pools, terraces, outdoor kitchens, docks, gardens.
- **Wellness and leisure:** gyms, spas, wine rooms, theaters.
- **Technology:** smart home systems, security, energy features.
- **Space for living and hosting:** guest suites, entertaining areas.

No single feature makes a home luxury. It is the combination, and how it all feels together.

## Is luxury real estate only about price?

No. Price sets the threshold, but buyers at the top of the market pay for rarity and experience. Two homes at the same price can feel completely different. The one with a better location, view or design usually wins.

## What is the difference between luxury and ultra-luxury?

Ultra-luxury usually refers to the very top of the market, often the top 1% of prices or homes above a high local threshold such as $10 million. These properties are rare, trade less often and are frequently marketed privately or to an international audience.

## How does luxury change from city to city?

In a lower-priced metro, a $1.2 million home with modern finishes and a large lot may be luxury. In Miami or Los Angeles, luxury often means waterfront, views or a top neighborhood, at several million dollars and up. Agents should always define luxury in terms of their own market.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a $1 million home considered luxury?

In some metros, yes. In Columbus, Ohio, the median luxury home was about $1.02 million in Redfin's data. In coastal metros like Miami or Los Angeles, $1 million is closer to mid-market.

### What percentage of homes are luxury?

It depends on the definition. Redfin uses the top 5% of homes in each metro, while some analysts use the top 10%.

### What is the most important feature of a luxury home?

Location. Views, waterfront access, privacy and neighborhood often matter more than size or finishes.
