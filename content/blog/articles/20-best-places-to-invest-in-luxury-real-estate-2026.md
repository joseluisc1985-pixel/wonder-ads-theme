## Which U.S. markets have the fastest luxury price growth?

According to Redfin's data for the three months ending April 2026, luxury prices (top 5% of each metro) rose most in:

| Metro | Luxury price change, year over year |
|---|---|
| Tampa, FL | +17.1% |
| Las Vegas, NV | +16.1% |
| Kansas City, MO | +15.2% |

By contrast, luxury prices fell slightly in Detroit (-2.4%), Cincinnati (-1.6%), New York (-0.6%) and Denver (-0.6%).

## 7 luxury markets to watch in 2026

### 1. Tampa, Florida

Tampa led the country in luxury price growth (+17.1%), and its luxury premium rose from 3.8 to 4.4 times a typical home, per Redfin. Waterfront living, no state personal income tax and steady in-migration support demand.

### 2. Las Vegas, Nevada

Las Vegas luxury prices rose 16.1%. Nevada has no state personal income tax, and the area's luxury communities and golf properties draw buyers from higher-cost states.

### 3. Kansas City, Missouri

A 15.2% luxury price gain makes Kansas City one of the surprise stories of 2026. Entry prices for luxury are far lower than on the coasts, which can appeal to investors looking for value.

### 4. Miami-Dade, Florida

Miami-Dade luxury single-family sales rose 32.5% to 1,000 in Q2 2026, with the average price up 21.9% to $3.61 million (The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties). Read our [Miami luxury market report](post:miami-luxury-real-estate-market-2026).

### 5. Palm Beach County, Florida

Palm Beach County recorded 2,534 luxury single-family sales in Q2 2026, up 24.2%, with an average price of $2.89 million. Compare it with Miami in our [Palm Beach vs. Miami guide](post:palm-beach-vs-miami-luxury-real-estate).

### 6. San Francisco, California

San Francisco was the only U.S. city in the top 10 of Knight Frank's Prime Global Cities Index, with prime prices up 5.0% in the year to Q2 2026.

### 7. Phoenix, Arizona

Phoenix's luxury premium rose from 4.3 to 4.8 times a typical home, with a median luxury price of about $2.23 million, per Redfin. That signals strong demand at the top end.

## How do you evaluate a luxury market yourself?

Use this checklist for any market you are considering:

1. **Price trend.** Are luxury prices rising, flat or falling?
2. **Sales activity.** Are luxury transactions growing?
3. **Supply.** How much luxury inventory is available, and is new construction coming?
4. **Demand drivers.** Jobs, migration, lifestyle, schools.
5. **Costs.** Taxes, insurance and HOA fees.
6. **Rules.** Rental restrictions if you plan to rent.
7. **Exit.** How long do comparable luxury homes take to sell?

Our [luxury real estate investing guide](post:luxury-real-estate-investing-guide) explains each factor.

## Does fast price growth mean a market is a good investment?

Not necessarily. Fast growth can mean strong demand, but it can also mean you are buying near a peak. Look at sales volume, inventory and long-term drivers alongside price changes.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best state to invest in luxury real estate?

Florida stands out in 2026 for both price growth (Tampa) and sales activity (Miami-Dade, Palm Beach), plus no state personal income tax. The best choice depends on your goals and budget.

### Is Las Vegas a good luxury market?

Las Vegas posted 16.1% luxury price growth in Redfin's latest data. As with any market, check supply, costs and your exit plan.

### Should I invest in New York luxury real estate in 2026?

New York luxury prices dipped slightly year over year, according to both Redfin and Knight Frank. Some investors see that as an opportunity, others as a reason to wait. Speak with a local expert.
