Email marketing works for luxury realtors because it reaches people who already know you: past clients, your sphere, other agents and serious buyers. The best emails are short, visual and useful. Send a listing announcement with a cinematic video, a monthly market note and a personal check-in a few times a year.

Here is what to send, plus templates you can adapt.

## Who should luxury realtors email?

Segment your list into groups so each email feels relevant:

| Segment | What they want |
|---|---|
| Past clients | Market updates, home value insight, personal check-ins |
| Sphere and friends | Occasional updates and stories |
| Agents and brokers | New listings and coming-soon previews |
| Active buyers | Homes that match their search |
| Potential sellers | Market insight and how you market homes |

## What emails should luxury agents send?

Send a few types on a steady rhythm:

1. **Listing launch.** The day a new listing goes live.
2. **Coming soon.** A teaser to agents and buyers before launch.
3. **Monthly market note.** One insight, one chart or number, one takeaway.
4. **Just sold.** A short story of how the home sold.
5. **Personal check-in.** A few times a year, no pitch.

## What does a great listing launch email look like?

Short, visual and focused on one action. Use a video thumbnail that links to the property page, three lines about the lifestyle, the key details and a button to book a private showing.

> **Subject:** Just listed: the bayfront home with the private dock
>
> Mornings on the water. Lunch by the pool. The skyline at night.
>
> [Video thumbnail: Watch the video] → links to your property page
>
> 6 beds · 7 baths · 70 ft of frontage · $8,950,000
>
> [Book a private showing]

## What subject lines work for luxury real estate emails?

Short, specific and curious subject lines work best. Avoid all caps and hype words.

- "Just listed: the home with the rooftop pool"
- "What $5M buys in [neighborhood] right now"
- "Luxury prices in [city]: one number to know"
- "A private preview, before it hits the market"
- "Sold in 21 days. Here's what we did."

Only use real results from your own sales.

## What should a monthly market email include?

One clear insight, one supporting number and what it means for the reader. Link to a longer post for those who want more. Our [2026 luxury market report](post:luxury-real-estate-market-2026) is an example of the kind of data that works.

## How often should you email your list?

Monthly is a good baseline for market notes, plus listing announcements as they happen. Watch unsubscribes and replies. If people engage, keep going. If they don't, send less and make each email more useful.

## What rules apply to real estate email marketing?

Get permission before adding people to marketing emails, include your business address and make unsubscribing easy. Rules vary by country and state, so check the laws that apply to you and your brokerage policy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is email marketing still effective for realtors?

Yes. Email reaches people who already know and trust you, and it's one of the few channels you own. It works best when emails are short and useful.

### Which email platform should I use?

Most agents use a CRM or email tool that supports segments and templates. Pick one you'll actually use every month.

### Should I send listings to my whole list?

Send new listings to agents and active buyers, and share only special listings with your wider sphere. Relevance keeps people subscribed.

### How do I grow my email list?

Offer something useful, like a monthly luxury market note, on your website and social profiles, and add every new contact with permission.
