A real estate video hook is the first line or image that makes someone stop scrolling. For luxury listings, the strongest hooks combine a striking visual, like the view or the most unusual room, with a short line that creates curiosity, such as "Nobody expects what's behind this door." You have about two seconds to earn the next ten.

Here are 25 hooks you can use, grouped by video type.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Open with your best visual, not the front door.
- Pair it with a short line on screen or in voiceover.
- Curiosity, contrast and specific numbers make strong hooks.
- Keep the promise. The video must deliver what the hook suggests.
- Test different hooks on the same video to see what works.
</div>

## What makes a good real estate video hook?

A good hook is specific, visual and fast. It shows something interesting right away and gives viewers a reason to keep watching. Vague openers like "Welcome to this beautiful home" lose people.

## Listing video hooks

1. "This is what $6 million looks like on the water."
2. "Nobody expects what's behind this door."
3. "Wait until you see the view from the bed."
4. "This kitchen has a secret."
5. "The best room in this house isn't inside."
6. "This home sold the owners in one minute. Here's that minute."
7. "Morning coffee hits different here."
8. "There are only a few homes like this in [city]."

## Neighborhood video hooks

9. "If you're moving to [city], start here."
10. "Three places in [neighborhood] locals don't share."
11. "I timed the drive from here to the beach."
12. "This is a perfect Saturday in [neighborhood]."
13. "Bayfront or high-rise? Here's the honest difference."

## Agent brand and seller hooks

19. "Here's everything I do before a home hits the market."
20. "This listing sat for months. Here's what we changed."
21. "Same room, one week apart."
22. "The biggest mistake luxury sellers make."
23. "What a listing launch week actually looks like."
24. "Pricing high to leave room? Here's what really happens."
25. "How I'd market a $10 million home."

Only use real results and numbers from your own experience.

## Which visual openers work best?

| Visual opener | Why it works |
|---|---|
| The view from inside | Instant "wow" and context |
| Doors opening to a terrace | Natural reveal |
| Twilight exterior | Mood and drama |
| A single standout detail | Curiosity |
| Water, pool or skyline motion | Movement stops the scroll |

## How do you test hooks?

Post the same video with two different first lines, or try different first shots across similar listings, and compare how much of the video people watch. Keep what works and drop what doesn't.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should a hook be?

One short sentence, or a few words on screen, in the first two seconds.

### Should the hook be spoken or written on screen?

Both work best together. Many viewers watch with sound off, so put key words on screen as captions.

### Can I reuse hooks?

Yes. Rotate your best hooks across listings and adjust the details to fit each home.
