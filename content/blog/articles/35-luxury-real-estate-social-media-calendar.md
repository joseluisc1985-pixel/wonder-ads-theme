A good social media calendar for luxury realtors rotates four types of content: listings, market insight, neighborhood life and personal brand. Posting four or five times a week on that rotation keeps your feed fresh, shows sellers how you market homes and gives buyers a reason to follow you.

Below is a 30-day plan you can copy.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Rotate listings, market insight, neighborhoods and personal brand.
- Plan a month at a time and batch-create content.
- Lead with vertical video on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.
- One listing video can fill a week of posts.
- Review what worked at the end of the month.
</div>

## What should luxury realtors post on social media?

Post around four content pillars:

| Pillar | Goal | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Listings | Show how you present homes | Cinematic listing reel |
| Market insight | Build authority | One-number market update |
| Neighborhood | Attract relocating buyers | A perfect Saturday in the area |
| Personal brand | Build trust | Behind the scenes of a launch |

## The 30-day calendar

**Week 1: Launch week**

- Day 1: Cinematic listing reel (hero video)
- Day 2: One-detail reveal from the same listing
- Day 3: Market update: one number, one takeaway
- Day 4: Neighborhood guide reel
- Day 5: Behind the scenes: preparing the listing
- Day 6: Carousel of the best photos
- Day 7: Rest or Stories only

- Day 8: Myth vs. fact for luxury sellers

- Day 10: Listing cutdown: the view
- Day 11: Client story (with permission)
- Day 12: Neighborhood hidden gems
- Day 13: Q&A in Stories
- Day 14: Rest

## How do you batch-create a month of content?

Set aside one planning session and one or two filming sessions a month. Gather listing videos, record market updates in one sitting and capture neighborhood clips on a single outing. Schedule posts with a planning tool so they go out on time.

## How do you measure what's working?

Track saves, shares, profile visits, messages and inquiries, not just likes. At the end of the month, repeat your top three posts' formats and drop the weakest.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should a luxury realtor post?

Four or five times a week is a realistic target, plus Stories. Consistency matters more than volume.

### Which platforms should I focus on?

Instagram and TikTok for discovery, Facebook for your local network and ads, YouTube for search. Pick two or three you can keep up with.

### Can I repurpose the same video across platforms?

Yes. Vertical videos work on Reels, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube Shorts. See our [TikTok guide](post:tiktok-for-luxury-realtors).

### What if I don't have listings this month?

Post market insight, neighborhood content and past listing stories. Your brand content keeps working between listings.
