Las Vegas is one of the strongest luxury real estate markets in the U.S. in 2026. Redfin reported luxury price growth of 16.1% in the three months ending April 2026, among the highest in the country, and 11.3% in the three months ending July. Nevada has no state personal income tax, which helps draw buyers from higher-tax states.

Here is what the data shows.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Las Vegas luxury prices rose 16.1% year over year (Redfin, three months ending April 2026).
- Growth continued at 11.3% in the three months ending July 2026, fifth in the nation.
- A local brokerage report put the median sold price of $1M+ homes at about $1.4 million over the 90 days to early September 2026.
- The overall Southern Nevada single-family median hit a record $490,000 in May and June 2026, per Las Vegas REALTORS.
- Nevada has no state personal income tax.
</div>

## How is the Las Vegas luxury market performing?

Strongly. Redfin, which defines luxury as the top 5% of each metro by estimated value, ranked Las Vegas second in the nation for luxury price growth in its May 2026 report (+16.1%) and fifth in its September report (+11.3%).

## Why are luxury buyers choosing Las Vegas?

Common reasons include Nevada's lack of a state personal income tax, lower luxury prices than coastal California, golf and guard-gated communities, newer construction and easy flights to major cities. Many buyers come from California and other higher-cost states.

## Which types of luxury homes are in demand in Las Vegas?

Guard-gated communities, golf course homes, custom estates with mountain or Strip views and high-rise residences all play a part. New construction is a bigger share of the luxury market than in older coastal cities.
