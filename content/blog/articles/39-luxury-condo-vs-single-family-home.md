A luxury condo suits buyers who want convenience, security, amenities and lock-and-leave living. A luxury single-family home suits buyers who want space, privacy, land and full control. In 2026, single-family homes have been the stronger performer in South Florida: Miami-Dade luxury house prices rose 21.9% in Q2, while luxury condo prices rose 4.9%.

Here is how they compare.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Condos offer amenities, services and less maintenance.
- Single-family homes offer privacy, land and control.
- Condos come with HOA fees and building rules. Houses come with full upkeep.
- In Q2 2026, South Florida luxury single-family prices outpaced condos.
- The right choice depends on lifestyle, how often you'll be there and long-term plans.
</div>

## What are the main differences between luxury condos and houses?

| | Luxury condo | Luxury single-family home |
|---|---|---|
| Lifestyle | Amenities, services, lock-and-leave | Space, privacy, gardens, pools |
| Maintenance | Mostly handled by the building | Owner's responsibility |
| Costs | HOA or condo fees, special assessments | Insurance, maintenance, staff |
| Rules | Building rules on renovations, rentals, pets | Fewer restrictions (HOA may apply) |
| Security | Staffed entry, controlled access | Depends on the property |
| Views | Often elevated city or water views | Ground-level views, waterfront access |

## How are luxury condos and houses performing in 2026?

In South Florida, houses have led. The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties' Q2 2026 Luxury Market Report showed:

| Q2 2026 | Single-family avg. price | Condo avg. price |
|---|---|---|
| Miami-Dade | $3.61M (+21.9%) | $2.83M (+4.9%) |
| Broward | $2.06M (+3.7%) | $1.94M (+5.6%) |
| Palm Beach | $2.89M (+4.6%) | $2.39M (-7.1%) |
| Five-county region | $2.63M (+7.2%) | $2.37M (+0.3%) |

Condo sales still grew strongly, up 23.5% across the region, so demand is healthy even where prices were flat.

## Who should buy a luxury condo?

Second-home owners, frequent travelers, downsizers and buyers who value services, security and amenities like gyms, pools and concierge. Condos also make sense for buyers who want views and a city location without managing a large property.

## Who should buy a luxury single-family home?

Families, buyers who want outdoor space, privacy and room for guests, and those who want full control over renovations and how they use the property. Waterfront houses with private docks are especially sought after.

## What costs should condo buyers check?

Check monthly fees, what they cover, the building's reserves, any planned special assessments and insurance. Rental and renovation rules matter too. Our article on [pre-construction luxury condos](post:pre-construction-luxury-condos) covers new buildings.

## Which holds value better?

Over time, land and scarcity tend to support value, which favors well-located single-family homes, especially on the water. Prime condos in sought-after buildings can also hold value well. Local data and the specific building matter more than general rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are luxury condos a good investment?

They can be, especially in strong buildings and locations. Fees, assessments and rules affect returns. See our [investing guide](post:luxury-real-estate-investing-guide).

### Why did Palm Beach condo prices fall?

The Q2 2026 report showed Palm Beach luxury condo prices down 7.1% on average, even as sales rose 26.6%. Averages can shift with the mix of units sold.

### Do condos have higher insurance costs?

Owners usually insure their unit interior, while the building insures the structure through fees. Total costs vary by building and location.

### Which is better for a second home?

Many second-home buyers choose condos for lock-and-leave convenience, but some prefer houses for privacy and space.
