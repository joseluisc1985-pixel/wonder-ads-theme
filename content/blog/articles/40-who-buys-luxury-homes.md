Luxury home buyers in 2026 are mostly experienced, repeat buyers, many relocating from higher-cost or higher-tax states, plus second-home and international buyers. The National Association of Realtors' 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers found the median age of repeat buyers reached a record 62, and 88% of buyers used an agent, which shows how much trust and guidance still matter.

Here are the main buyer types and how to reach them.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Repeat buyers dominate the luxury market. NAR's median repeat-buyer age hit 62.
- Relocating buyers from New York and California are fueling Florida's luxury growth, per Redfin.
- 88% of buyers purchased through an agent or broker (NAR).
- Second-home and international buyers often shortlist homes remotely.
- Each buyer type responds to different marketing.
</div>

## What does the typical home buyer look like in 2026?

NAR's 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers reported record highs for buyer age: 59 for all buyers, 40 for first-time buyers and 62 for repeat buyers. First-time buyers fell to a survey low of 21%. Most buyers (88%) used an agent or broker, and 85% ranked agents as their most useful information source.

Luxury buyers skew toward experienced repeat buyers with equity from previous homes.

## Who are the main types of luxury buyers?

| Buyer type | What they want | How to reach them |
|---|---|---|
| Relocating professionals and families | Lifestyle, schools, tax benefits | Ads in their home cities, neighborhood videos |
| Move-up and downsizing repeat buyers | Space or simplicity, quality, location | Email, referrals, market insight |
| Second-home buyers | Lock-and-leave, views, amenities | Social media, video, remote touring |
| International buyers | Safety, lifestyle, investment | Captioned video, global portals, referrals |
| Investors | Appreciation, rental potential | Data-driven content |

## Why are so many luxury buyers relocating?

In Florida, Redfin attributes much of the luxury growth to buyers from New York and California drawn by the favorable tax environment, warm weather and waterfront lifestyle. Florida has no state personal income tax. Similar patterns show up in Texas, Nevada and Tennessee, which also don't tax wages.

## How do luxury buyers search for homes?

Most start online, often on a phone, and many shortlist homes before visiting. Video, social media and property websites play a big role. But buyers still lean on agents for guidance, negotiation and access, which is why your presentation matters as much as the home's.

## How should agents market to luxury buyers?

1. **Lead with lifestyle.** See [lifestyle marketing in luxury real estate](post:lifestyle-marketing-luxury-real-estate).
2. **Make remote touring easy** with cinematic video and detailed property pages.
3. **Reach buyers where they live now**, through targeted ads and agent referrals.
4. **Respect privacy and time.** High-end buyers value discretion and efficiency.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the average age of a luxury home buyer?

NAR doesn't publish a luxury-only figure, but its 2025 report put the median repeat buyer at a record 62. Luxury buyers are often repeat buyers.

### Do luxury buyers use real estate agents?

Most buyers do. NAR found 88% of buyers purchased through an agent or broker in its 2025 report.

### Where are luxury buyers moving from?

In Florida, Redfin points to New York and California as major sources of affluent buyers.
