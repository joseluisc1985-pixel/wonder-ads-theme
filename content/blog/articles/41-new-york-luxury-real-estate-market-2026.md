New York's luxury real estate market in 2026 is a story of two speeds. Metro-wide, luxury prices slipped slightly: Redfin reported a 0.6% decline in the three months ending April 2026, and Knight Frank recorded -0.4% for prime New York over the year to Q2. At the very top, though, Manhattan contracts for $10 million to $20 million homes jumped 38.6% year over year in Q2 2026, according to Compass.

Here is what the data says.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- New York's median luxury home was about $4.38 million (Redfin, three months ending May 2026).
- Luxury prices dipped 0.6% year over year in Redfin's spring data. Knight Frank showed -0.4% for prime.
- Manhattan $10M to $20M contracts rose 38.6% and $20M+ contracts rose 25% in Q2 2026 (Compass).
- Manhattan's overall median price rose 6.3% to $1,275,000 in Q2 2026.
- New York's luxury premium held steady at 5.5 times a typical home.
</div>

## How is the New York luxury market performing in 2026?

Prices are roughly flat overall, while activity at the ultra-high end is strong. Redfin defines luxury as the top 5% of each metro by estimated value. New York was one of only four major metros where luxury prices fell in Redfin's May 2026 report, down 0.6%.

## Why are New York luxury prices flat while the top end is busy?

Different measures capture different slices of the market. Metro-wide luxury data covers the top 5% across a huge region, while Manhattan contract data highlights the trophy segment. Strong activity among the wealthiest buyers can coexist with flat prices in the broader luxury tier.

## How does New York compare with Florida?

Florida markets have posted much faster luxury price growth. Miami's luxury median rose 18% in Redfin's report for the three months ending July 2026, and Redfin noted many affluent Florida buyers come from New York. ## Frequently asked questions

### Are New York luxury home prices falling?

Slightly, by some measures. Redfin reported -0.6% and Knight Frank -0.4% year over year, which is close to flat.

### What is the median luxury home price in New York?

About $4.38 million in Redfin's data for the three months ending May 2026.

### Is Manhattan's luxury market recovering?

The top end had a strong Q2 2026, with $10M to $20M contracts up 38.6% according to Compass.

### Is New York or Miami a better luxury market?

They serve different buyers. Miami has shown faster recent price growth, while New York offers a deep, global trophy market.
