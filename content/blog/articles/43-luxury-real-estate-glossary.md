This luxury real estate glossary defines the terms that come up most often when buying, selling or marketing high-end homes, in plain English. Bookmark it, or share it with clients who are new to the luxury market.

**Key takeaways**

- Luxury deals use a few terms you won't hear in typical sales.

## Market and pricing terms

**Luxury home:** A home in the top tier of its local market, often defined as the top 5% to 10% of prices. See [what is considered a luxury home](post:what-is-considered-a-luxury-home).

**Ultra-luxury:** The very top of the market, often the top 1% or homes above a high local threshold.

**Prime property:** The most desirable homes in a city's best locations, a term used in global market reports.

**Luxury premium:** How many times more a typical luxury home costs than a typical home in the same area.

**Comparable sales (comps):** Recent sales of similar homes used to estimate value.

**Price per square foot:** Price divided by living area. Less reliable at the top of the market.

**Days on market (DOM):** How long a listing has been active.

**Absorption rate:** How quickly available homes are selling in a market.

## Listing and marketing terms

**Off-market listing:** A home sold privately without public listing.

**Pocket listing:** A listing an agent markets privately to select buyers or agents. MLS rules on this vary.

**Coming soon:** A status for homes about to be listed, where allowed by the MLS.

**Broker open:** An event for agents to preview a new listing. See [luxury open house ideas](post:luxury-open-house-ideas).

**Positioning:** The one-sentence story of who a home is for and why it's rare.

**Lifestyle marketing:** Marketing that shows the life a home offers. See [lifestyle marketing](post:lifestyle-marketing-luxury-real-estate).

**Cinematic listing video:** A short, story-driven video with music, voiceover and captions.

**Single property website:** A dedicated page for one listing.

**Virtual staging:** Digitally adding furniture to photos, which usually requires disclosure.

**Twilight photography:** Exterior photos taken at dusk.

## Financing and legal terms

**Jumbo loan:** A mortgage above the conforming loan limit. See [jumbo loans explained](post:jumbo-loans-explained).

**Conforming loan limit:** The maximum loan size eligible for purchase by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. For 2026, $832,750 for most one-unit homes.

**Proof of funds:** Documents showing a buyer can pay cash.

**Escrow:** A neutral third party holding funds or documents until closing.

**Title insurance:** Protection against problems with ownership records.

**Appraisal:** A licensed appraiser's estimate of a home's value, usually required by lenders.

**Closing costs:** Fees and expenses paid at closing, such as title, escrow, recording and lender fees.

**Contingency:** A condition in a contract, such as inspection or financing.

**Assignment:** Transferring a purchase contract to another buyer, common in pre-construction.

**LLC purchase:** Buying property through a limited liability company. See [buying through an LLC](post:buying-luxury-home-through-llc).

## Investment and tax terms

**1031 exchange:** A U.S. tax rule that lets investors defer capital gains tax by reinvesting in like-kind property. See [1031 exchanges for luxury real estate](post:1031-exchange-luxury-real-estate).

**FIRPTA:** A U.S. law that generally requires withholding when a foreign person sells U.S. real estate.

**Capital gains:** Profit from selling a property.

**Carrying costs:** Ongoing costs of owning a property: taxes, insurance, fees, maintenance.

**Cap rate:** Net operating income divided by property value, a rental return measure.

**Appreciation:** Increase in property value over time.

## Property feature terms

**Frontage:** The length of a property along water, a road or a view.

**Seawall:** A structure protecting waterfront land from the water.

**Deep-water access:** Water deep enough for larger boats.

**Guard-gated community:** A neighborhood with staffed security entry.

**Lock-and-leave:** A home easy to leave for long periods, often a condo.

**Smart home:** Connected systems for lighting, climate, security and entertainment.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between off-market and pocket listings?

The terms overlap. Both describe homes marketed privately. MLS rules on private marketing vary, so check yours.

### What does "luxury premium" mean?

It compares the price of a typical luxury home to a typical home in the same market. Redfin reported a national premium of 3.6 times in 2026.

### What is a conforming loan limit?

The largest loan Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac will buy. Loans above it are jumbo loans.
