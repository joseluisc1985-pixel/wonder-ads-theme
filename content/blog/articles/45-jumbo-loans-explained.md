A jumbo loan is a mortgage larger than the conforming loan limit set each year by the Federal Housing Finance Agency. For 2026, the baseline limit for one-unit homes is $832,750, rising to $1,249,125 in high-cost areas. Because jumbo loans can't be sold to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac, lenders usually ask for stronger credit, larger down payments and more cash reserves.

## What is a jumbo loan?

A jumbo loan is a non-conforming mortgage: it's too large to meet the limits for loans backed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. Lenders keep these loans or sell them privately, so they set their own requirements.

## What are the 2026 conforming loan limits?

| 2026 limit (one-unit homes) | Amount |
|---|---|
| Baseline | $832,750 |
| High-cost area ceiling | $1,249,125 |

The baseline rose by $26,250 from 2025. Limits vary by county, so check the figure for your area.

## What do lenders require for a jumbo loan?

Requirements vary, but lenders commonly look at:

- **Credit score:** usually stronger than for conforming loans.
- **Debt-to-income ratio:** your monthly debts compared with income.
- **Down payment:** often larger than for conforming loans.
- **Cash reserves:** months of payments in savings or investments.
- **Documentation:** tax returns, assets, sometimes two appraisals for very large loans.

## How do jumbo loan rates compare?

Jumbo rates can be higher, similar or even lower than conforming rates depending on the market and lender. Private banks sometimes offer better terms to clients who hold assets with them. Compare offers from several lenders.

## Should luxury buyers use a jumbo loan or pay cash?

Many luxury buyers pay cash, others finance to keep money invested elsewhere. The right choice depends on interest rates, returns on other investments, liquidity and taxes. Talk with your financial advisor.

## How does jumbo financing affect the buying process?

Get pre-approved early. Sellers of high-end homes want proof you can close. Expect a more detailed underwriting process and a careful appraisal. See [how to buy a luxury home](post:luxury-home-buying-process).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the jumbo loan limit for 2026?

Any loan above the conforming limit is jumbo. For 2026, that's $832,750 in most areas and up to $1,249,125 in high-cost areas for one-unit homes.

### How much down payment do I need for a jumbo loan?

It depends on the lender, loan size and your finances. Compare offers.

### Can I get a jumbo loan for a second home or investment property?

Many lenders offer jumbo loans for second homes and investment properties, often with stricter requirements.

### Are jumbo loans harder to get?

They usually have stricter requirements than conforming loans, but well-qualified buyers can find competitive options.
