Nine U.S. states don't tax wage income: Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington and Wyoming. For high earners, that can mean big savings, which is one reason luxury demand has been strong in Florida, Nevada and Texas. Redfin's 2026 luxury reports point to buyers from New York and California moving to Florida partly for its favorable tax environment.

Here is what that means for luxury buyers and agents. This is educational content, not tax advice.

## How do income taxes affect luxury real estate demand?

For high earners and retirees with large incomes, state income tax can add up to a meaningful annual cost. Moving to a no-income-tax state can free up money, which some buyers put toward a home. Redfin's September 2026 report cited Florida's favorable tax environment, warm weather and waterfront lifestyle as draws for affluent buyers from New York and California.

## Which no-income-tax markets are growing fastest?

In Redfin's data for the three months ending July 2026, Miami led the nation in luxury price growth (+18%), followed by Tampa (+15.4%), with Las Vegas fifth (+11.3%). ## What other costs should buyers compare?

No income tax doesn't mean low costs overall. Compare:

- **Property taxes**, which can be high in some no-income-tax states.
- **Insurance**, especially in coastal Florida and other storm-prone areas.
- **Sales taxes** and other local taxes.
- **HOA fees** and maintenance.

Our [luxury investing guide](post:luxury-real-estate-investing-guide) covers carrying costs in detail.

## Does moving states automatically change your tax residency?

No. Changing residency involves where you live, work and keep ties, and your former state may review it. Talk to a tax professional before you move.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which states have no income tax in 2026?

Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington and Wyoming don't tax wage income. Washington taxes certain capital gains.

### Why are wealthy buyers moving to Florida?

Common reasons include no state personal income tax, warm weather, waterfront living and a growing business scene, as noted in Redfin's 2026 reports.

### Are property taxes higher in no-income-tax states?

Some, like Texas, have relatively high property taxes. Compare the total cost of ownership.

### Do I pay federal taxes in a no-income-tax state?

Yes. Federal income tax applies everywhere in the U.S.
