Managing a luxury property well means protecting its condition, security and value, especially when you're not there. The essentials are a trusted property manager or caretaker, a regular maintenance schedule, reliable security and smart-home monitoring, a vetted vendor list and, for rentals, a guest experience that earns great reviews.

Here is how to set it up. This is educational content, not financial advice.

<div class="key-takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- A good manager or caretaker is the backbone of remote ownership.
- Preventive maintenance protects both value and insurance.
- Security and monitoring systems give peace of mind.
- Build a vetted list of vendors before you need them.
- For rentals, presentation and guest experience drive income.
</div>

## Do you need a property manager for a luxury home?

If you live far away or rent the property, usually yes. A manager or caretaker handles inspections, vendors, emergencies and guests. Owners who live nearby may manage themselves with a strong vendor list.

## What should a luxury property maintenance schedule include?

| Frequency | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Walk-through, pool, landscaping, mail and deliveries |
| Monthly | Systems check, pest control, smart-home and security test |
| Seasonally | HVAC service, roof and gutter check, storm prep in coastal areas |
| Yearly | Deep inspection, exterior paint touch-ups, appliance service |

Keep records. They help with insurance and resale.

## How do you keep a luxury home secure when you're away?

Use monitored security systems, cameras, smart locks and water-leak sensors, and have someone check the home regularly. In storm-prone areas, have a plan for shutters, generators and post-storm inspections.

## How do you build a reliable vendor list?

Find and vet providers before you need them: pool, landscaping, HVAC, electrician, plumber, roofer, housekeeping, handyman and, for waterfront homes, dock and seawall specialists. Ask other owners and your agent for referrals.

## How do you manage a luxury rental?

Focus on guest experience: spotless cleaning, clear check-in, high-quality amenities and fast responses. Refresh furnishings regularly and keep photos and video up to date. See our [luxury vacation rental guide](post:luxury-vacation-rental-investment).

## How does good management protect value?

Well-maintained homes avoid costly repairs, insure more easily and show better when it's time to sell. Documented maintenance also reassures buyers. See [how to value a luxury home](post:how-to-value-a-luxury-home).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does luxury property management cost?

Fees vary by location, services and whether the home is rented. Rental management is often a percentage of income.

### What's the difference between a property manager and a caretaker?

A property manager typically handles business tasks like vendors, rentals and finances, while a caretaker focuses on hands-on upkeep. Some do both.

### How often should someone check a vacant second home?

Many owners arrange weekly checks, more often during storm season or extreme weather.

### Can smart-home technology replace a manager?

It helps with monitoring, but someone still needs to respond to problems and manage vendors.
